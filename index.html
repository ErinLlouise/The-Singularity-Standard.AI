About this tracker

Composite score is the unweighted mean of four benchmarks: MMLU, GPQA Diamond, SWE-bench Verified, and ARC-AGI. Numbers come from each lab's published model cards and system cards.

Caveat: benchmark conditions vary across labs (prompting, eval version, tool access). Treat the ranking as directional, not definitive.

To refresh the closed-lab numbers, invoke the agi-tracker-updater subagent in Claude Code.
