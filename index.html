About this tracker

Benchmarks view — composite is the unweighted mean of four academic benchmarks: MMLU, GPQA Diamond, SWE-bench Verified, and ARC-AGI-1. Numbers come from each lab's published model cards and system cards. Missing scores are skipped when averaging rather than treated as zero.

Arena Elo view — Elo ratings from arena.ai/leaderboard/text, a human-preference leaderboard. Users compare two anonymous model responses side-by-side and pick a winner; ratings are derived from pairwise outcomes across millions of votes. Each lab ranks by its top model's Elo across the ~360 models listed; the ± value is the margin of error on each rating.

Caveat: academic benchmarks measure reasoning correctness on fixed tasks; Arena Elo measures human preference on open-ended chat. They reward different things — a lab can lead on one and lag on the other. Treat either ranking as directional, not definitive.

To refresh data, invoke the agi-tracker-updater subagent in Claude Code, or wait for the daily 9am routine.
